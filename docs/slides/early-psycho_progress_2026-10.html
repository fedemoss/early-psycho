GEC

Linear Hopf (a = −0.02), pseudo-gradient fit to FC + FS(τ = 1 TR). Fit r(FC) ≈ 0.61.

P2H / P2O

Inject extra noise at node j; measure how the simulated FC moves toward the healthy mean (P2H) or away from baseline (P2O).

Perturbability

FDT-based response map per node (Deco / Kringelbach). Also GEC asymmetry ‖C − Cᵀ‖.

v1 · perturbation and symptoms

Perturbation measures did not track PANSS

Direct correlation

max(P2O) vs PANSS: r = 0.09 (p = 0.38)
min(P2H) vs PANSS: r = −0.13 (p = 0.22)

Same null for P2H/P2O vs the model’s per-subject error.

Perturb → predicted PANSS

Node minimising predicted PANSS = node minimising P2H in 0 / 92 subjects. Weak rank effect (44.7 vs 50.5, p = 0.004).

Used the leaky model, and TR = 2.0 instead of 0.8.

One region everywhere

argmin(P2H) = region 26 (LH salience lateral PFC) in 78 / 92 patients and in 40 / 48 controls.

Group effects unstable

GEC asymmetry: HC < AP in the 10-subject pilot (0.052 vs 0.061), HC > AP in the full run (0.064 vs 0.049).

Audit · P2H

The “best” perturbation was the most damaging one

P2Hj = sim(FCpert j, FCHC) − sim(FCbase, FCHC)

  • Higher P2H = the perturbation moves the subject toward healthy
  • v1 notebooks used argmin(P2H) as “closest to healthy”: that is the node whose perturbation hurts similarity most
  • P2H < 0 for essentially every node and subject: no single-node noise injection improves anyone
  • Region 26 won in controls too, so it reflects the model and atlas, not the patients

v2 conventions

best node = argmax

Healthy reference computed leave-one-out for controls. Added Mana-style perturbations of the bifurcation parameter aj.

03

New hypotheses (v2)

Literature-driven questions, all tested with the fixed model and leak-free statistics.

v2 · hypotheses

Four questions from the literature

H1 · hierarchy

More non-equilibrium in patients

Acero-Pousa 2025: higher FDT deviation (“hierarchy”) in chronic schizophrenia. Same in early psychosis? Plus model-free irreversibility.

Not supported: no FDT difference; irreversibility goes the opposite way (lower).

H2 · Mana priors

Restorative regions rank high

Mana 2023: perturbing OFC, posterior cingulate, parahippocampal cortex restores healthy dynamics. Top of our P2H maps?

Not supported: Mana sets sit at chance in every map.

H3 · higher-order

Less synergy, more redundancy

Tarchi 2026: schizophrenia loses synergy. Same here, and can the Hopf model reproduce it?

Not supported: non-significant trend the other way; the model makes no synergy.

H4 · symptoms

Features predict symptoms

Do any features, or literature priors (Keane 2025), predict PANSS or YMRS beyond the AP/NAP difference?

Not supported: all 13 feature sets at permutation-null level.

Verdict · H1 not supported (as stated)

The Acero-Pousa measure shows no group difference, and the model-free effect runs the opposite way: patients’ dynamics are closer to equilibrium, not further. The reversed effect itself is robust and is now our main finding.

Irreversibility: FDR q < 0.001, at lags 1–3 and in all 7 networks (NAP g −0.79 to −0.90).

Model asymmetry tracks empirical irreversibility (ρ = 0.91). FDT deviation: no group difference (p = 0.27).

Opposite direction to Acero-Pousa (chronic SZ). No symptom correlation survives FDR.

Verdict · H4 not supported

Every feature set is at permutation-null level (all p ≥ 0.16). PANSS total and YMRS may be the wrong targets.

Protocol

Repeated 10-fold CV; selection, scaling and ridge α inside folds; AP/NAP means removed in-fold; 200-permutation null of the full pipeline, shuffling within group.

R² never exceeds the group-only model. Consistent with Foster 2025: PANSS total is not predictable in early psychosis, but its factors were.

04

Where we stand, and next steps

One robust group effect, honest nulls for symptoms, and a clear list of things to do.

Summary

What we know now

robust

Lower irreversibility in psychosis

g ≈ −0.9 (NAP), −0.6 (AP); every network and lag; survives FC adjustment; mirrored by GEC asymmetry.

improved

A correct, better-fitting model

Six fixes; fit 0.61 → 0.77; code reorganised in src/ with notebooks per study.

trend

Less redundancy, more synergy

p ≈ 0.07–0.10; the linear model produces no synergy at all.

null

No symptom prediction, no Mana enrichment

The v1 R² = 0.31 was leakage; PANSS total and YMRS not predictable; Mana sets at chance.

v2 pipeline rerun from src/ on 22 Oct; all results reproduced21
